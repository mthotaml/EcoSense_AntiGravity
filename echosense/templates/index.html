Music DNA Affinity

Music DNA Affinity measures how closely a track matches your long-term musical taste vector built from your Spotify library.

Calculation Formula:
DNA Affinity = (0.60 × Artist Affinity) + (0.40 × Category Fit)
  • Artist Affinity (60% weight): Base score of 0.85 for your top artists on Spotify, boosted by +0.10 for recent plays (up to 1.0).
  • Category/Genre Fit (40% weight): Match score against your primary listened categories (e.g. Deep Focus, Nature Soundscapes, Eco Lo-Fi Beats).
